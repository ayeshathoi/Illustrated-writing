The studio runs on your computer

To upload books, open a terminal in the project folder and run:

python3 server.py

Then go to http://localhost:8000/admin. When you're happy, commit and push to publish.

    Painted meadow (default)
    A soft landscape (meadow, forest, sea, flowers) works best. Use photos you took or free ones from Unsplash or Pexels, since images on Pinterest usually belong to other people.

    Publish online

    Changes here are saved to the books/ folder. To put them on your live site, push them to GitHub:

    git add -A
git commit -m "New pages"
git push

    Make something lovely

    Pick a book on the left, or start a new one. You can drop in page images (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF) or a whole PDF, and each PDF page becomes a page in the book.
